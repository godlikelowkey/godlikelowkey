Your 45-Minute
Dumbbell Routine

A full-body dumbbell workout using 8–55 lb weights. Work through each exercise in order, rest between sets, and move to the next.

Exercises
60s
Rest / set
90s

Weights used
Warm-up — 5 min
Arm Circles & Shoulder Rolls
2 × 30 sec · bodyweight

Stand with feet shoulder-width apart. Extend arms out to the sides and make small circles forward for 15 seconds, then reverse. Follow with slow shoulder rolls — forward 5 times, backward 5 times. Keep your chest open and neck relaxed.

60 sec rest
Goblet Squat — slow
2 × 8 reps · 15 lb

Hold one 15 lb dumbbell vertically at your chest with both hands. Feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out. Take 3 seconds to lower until thighs are parallel, pause at the bottom, then drive through your heels to stand. This is a movement primer — focus on depth and control, not speed.

60 sec rest
Romanian Deadlift — hinge practice
2 × 8 reps · 20 lb each

Hold a dumbbell in each hand in front of your thighs. Hinge at the hips — push them back as you lower the weights down your legs, keeping your back flat and knees soft. Feel the stretch in your hamstrings, then drive your hips forward to return. Think: close a car door with your butt.

Upper Push — 13 min
Dumbbell Shoulder Press
3 × 10 reps · 25 lb each

Sit or stand with a dumbbell in each hand at shoulder height, palms facing forward. Press straight overhead until arms are fully extended, then lower slowly back to shoulders. Don't arch your lower back — brace your core throughout. If standing, keep a slight knee bend.

60 sec rest
Floor Chest Press
3 × 10 reps · 35 lb each

Lie on your back with knees bent, a dumbbell in each hand. Start with elbows at 90°, upper arms resting on the floor. Press the weights up and slightly together, then lower until your upper arms touch the floor. The floor limits your range of motion — this is intentional and protects the shoulder.

60 sec rest
Lateral Raises
3 × 12 reps · 8 lb each

Stand with a light dumbbell in each hand at your sides. With a slight bend in the elbows, raise both arms out to the sides until they're at shoulder height — no higher. Lower slowly over 2 seconds. Lead with your elbows, not your wrists. If you're shrugging, the weight is too heavy.

Lower Body — 13 min
Goblet Squat
3 × 12 reps · 45 lb

Same movement as the warm-up, now with a heavier load. Hold the 45 lb dumbbell vertically at your chest. Squat deep — aim for thighs parallel or below. Drive your knees out over your toes as you lower, and squeeze your glutes at the top of each rep.

60 sec rest
Romanian Deadlift
3 × 10 reps · 55 lb each

Same hip-hinge pattern as the warm-up, now loaded. Hold 55 lb dumbbells, hinge until you feel a strong hamstring stretch — typically just below the knee. Keep the weights close to your legs the entire time. Squeeze your glutes as you return to standing. Avoid rounding your lower back.

60 sec rest
Reverse Lunge
3 × 10 reps / leg · 25 lb each

Hold a dumbbell in each hand at your sides. Step one foot back and lower your back knee toward the floor — front shin stays vertical. Push through your front heel to return to standing. Alternate legs or complete all reps on one side, then switch. Keep your torso upright throughout.

Upper Pull — 10 min
Bent-Over Row
3 × 10 reps · 35 lb each

Hinge forward at the hips until your torso is roughly 45°. Hold a dumbbell in each hand, arms hanging. Pull the weights toward your hips, driving your elbows back and squeezing your shoulder blades together at the top. Don't use momentum — lower with control over 2 seconds.

60 sec rest
Hammer Curl
3 × 12 reps · 20 lb each

Stand with a dumbbell in each hand, palms facing each other (neutral grip). Curl both weights toward your shoulders without letting your elbows drift forward. Lower fully between each rep. The neutral grip targets the brachialis and brachioradialis alongside the bicep for thicker-looking arms.

60 sec rest
Single-Arm Row
3 × 10 reps / side · 45 lb

Place one hand and same-side knee on a bench or chair for support. Hold the 45 lb dumbbell with your free hand. Row it up to your hip, keeping your elbow tight to your body. Think: starting a lawnmower. Lower fully, complete all reps on one side, then switch. Don't rotate your torso.

Core Finisher — 4 min
Russian Twist
2 × 20 reps (10/side) · 15 lb

Sit on the floor with knees bent, feet slightly raised. Hold the dumbbell with both hands at your chest. Lean back slightly until you feel your core engage, then rotate the weight from side to side — left and right counts as two reps. Move from your torso, not just your arms. Keep breathing.

60 sec rest
Farmer's Carry
2 × 30 sec · 55 lb each

Pick up the heaviest dumbbells and walk. Stand tall — shoulders back, core braced, eyes forward. Take controlled steps for 30 seconds without setting the weights down. This is harder than it looks. It builds grip strength, core stability, and total-body endurance all at once. The perfect finisher.

Notes
Rest times
60 seconds between sets, 90 seconds between sections. Extend to 2 minutes on heavier compound lifts (RDL, row) if needed — better to rest fully than grind through bad reps.
Weight scaling
Drop one weight tier if your form breaks down. On the RDL and shoulder press especially, control beats load every time.
Tempo
Aim for 2 seconds down, a brief pause, 2 seconds up on most movements. Slow eccentrics (the lowering phase) are where most of the muscle growth happens.
