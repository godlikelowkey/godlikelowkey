03
PROTOCOL NOTES
01
Rest periods
60 seconds between sets. 90 seconds between blocks. Extend if form degrades — better to rest than rush.
02
Weight scaling
Drop one weight tier if form breaks down, especially on RDL and shoulder press. Control matters more than load.
03
Tempo
Keep a 2–1–2 tempo on most lifts — 2 seconds down, pause, 2 seconds up. Slow eccentrics build more muscle.
